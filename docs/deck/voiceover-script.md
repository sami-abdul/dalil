# Dalil demo: voiceover script and recording steps

The video is `dalil-demo.mp4` in this folder: about 2 minutes 20 seconds, captioned, no sound. Read each block while its caption is on screen. The caption it goes with is in quotes, so you can follow along without timestamps. Speak at an easy pace; each block fits its scene with room to spare. If you fall behind, drop the second sentence of a block.

## Script

**1. Opening.** Caption: "Dalil answers questions about UAE government services..."

> Residents in the UAE ask the same questions every day. The answers are spread over a dozen government sites, and a wrong one costs someone a fine. This is Dalil. It answers from the official sources, and a person approves every answer before it goes out.

**2. Track.** Caption: "Open Agent Hackathon 2026, Track 02..."

> We built it for Track 02, the agent that can explain why.

**3. The question.** Caption: "A resident asks: can a Golden Visa holder sponsor their parents?"

> Here is a real question: can a Golden Visa holder sponsor their parents?

**4. The pipeline.** Caption: "Dalil screens the question, recalls past reviews from Meterless H-MEM..."

> Dalil screens it for personal data, checks Meterless H-MEM for past reviews of similar questions, then searches a thousand passages from official documents with NVIDIA Nemotron embeddings. Nemotron 3 Nano writes the draft.

**5. The wait.** Caption: "On this server's CPU an answer takes about 40 seconds..."

> Everything runs on our own server, on the CPU, so we've cut the wait.

**6. The draft.** Caption: "The draft is back..."

> Every sentence in the draft points at a numbered source.

**7. A citation.** Caption: "Click a citation and the quoted words are highlighted..."

> Click one, and you see the exact words, highlighted on the official page. The checker found them there word for word.

**8. The cut.** Caption: "The model also wrote that Golden Visa holders can sponsor their parents..."

> And here's the important part. The model also wrote that Golden Visa holders can sponsor their parents. The source says spouses and children. So the checker cut that sentence, and it will never reach the resident.

**9. The audit trail.** Caption: "The audit trail records every step..."

> Every step is in the audit trail: the screening, the memory recall, the search, the draft and the check. Anyone can replay why an answer went out.

**10. Zetaris.** Caption: "For numbers, Dalil queries open data through Zetaris..."

> For numbers, Dalil queries open data through Zetaris. The documents and four government open datasets sit in one Zetaris database.

**11. Zetaris check.** Caption: "When an answer states a number from open data..."

> When an answer uses one of those numbers, it cites the query, and the checker reruns it before the sentence can stay.

**12. Approval.** Caption: "Nothing goes out until a person approves it..."

> Nothing goes out until a person approves it, and only the sentences that passed are sent.

**13. Memory.** Caption: "The decision and the passages it cited go to Meterless H-MEM's trust ledger..."

> The decision goes into Meterless H-MEM's trust ledger, with the passages it cited. If a reviewer rejects an answer, those passages stay out of the next answer to a similar question.

**14. Close.** Caption: "Dalil. Answers about UAE government services that you can check."

> Dalil. Answers about government services that you can check. The code is on GitHub.

## Recording steps, about 20 minutes

1. Download `dalil-demo.mp4` and open it in QuickTime. Do one silent read-through with the script to get the pace.
2. Start Loom with "Screen + mic" or "Screen only" plus mic, recording the QuickTime window. Use headphones so nothing echoes.
3. Press play in QuickTime and read the script along with the captions. Stop Loom when the closing caption ends.
4. Watch it back once. It must run between 1 and 4 minutes; it will be about 2:20.
5. Set the Loom link so anyone with it can view, open it in a private window to check, and send the link to HQ for `video_url`.

If there is no time to record, upload `dalil-demo.mp4` as it is, unlisted on YouTube or Loom; the captions carry the whole story.
