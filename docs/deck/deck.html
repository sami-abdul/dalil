Track 02, The Agent That Can Explain Why

Problem and usersOne wrong answer costs a resident a fine

  • Residents ask the same questions every day: sponsoring family, renewing an Emirates ID, notice periods, end-of-service gratuity.
  • The answers sit across service pages, federal laws and fee tables from different authorities.
  • A wrong answer means a fine or a rejected application, so a service team cannot let a chatbot answer unchecked.
  • Who uses Dalil: service-centre staff who answer residents, and the residents who get the reviewed answer.
Dalil2

SolutionSeven steps, and a person signs off

1. AskA resident's question comes in.
2. ScreenPersonal data is redacted before storage. Requests to get around the rules are refused.
3. Search1,024 passages from 45 official documents, plus open data through Zetaris.
4. DraftNVIDIA Nemotron writes the answer. Every sentence points at a source.
5. CheckQuotes must match word for word, numbers must be in the evidence. Failing sentences are cut.
6. ApproveA person reads it with every quote highlighted on its page. Nothing goes out before.
7. RememberThe decision goes to Meterless H-MEM. A rejection changes the next similar answer.

Sponsor technologyHow Zetaris and Meterless are used

Zetaris

Documents, pages, passages and four Bayanat open datasets live in one Lightning database.

A number from open data is cited as a Zetaris query. The checker reruns that query before the sentence may stay.

Zetaris proves every passage equals its slice of the page: 1,024 of 1,024.

Meterless H-MEM

Every approval and rejection lands in H-MEM's append-only trust ledger, with the passages it cited.

Before drafting, Dalil recalls reviews of similar questions and leaves out passages a reviewer rejected.

NVIDIA Nemotron drafts and embeds, running on the team's own server6

What is newA citation you can rerun

  • A quote maps to an exact character span on its page. A number maps to a Zetaris query.
  • The checker trusts neither the model's quotes nor its SQL. Dalil writes each quote itself and refuses a number the evidence lacks.
  • A reviewer's rejection changes the next answer to a similar question.
Model draft: "Golden visa holders can sponsor their parents."
Cut by the checker: the cited source names spouses and children.
